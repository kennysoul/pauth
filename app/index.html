Oauth KASS – 内部身份认证服务

Oauth KASS is an identity and access management service designed for internal team use. It authenticates users via WebAuthn Passkey, Google OAuth 2.0, and Microsoft OAuth 2.0, and provides OpenID Connect (OIDC) single sign‑on (SSO) to internal applications. The service collects only the minimum information necessary for authentication and never uses Google user data for advertising, marketing, or third‑party sharing.
